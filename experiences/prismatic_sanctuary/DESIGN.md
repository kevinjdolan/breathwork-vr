# Prismatic Sanctuary — flowing geometric trance

Eight minutes of slow forward optic flow through 128 floating chromatic objects and a 16,384-point woven vault. The tracked camera remains physically correct: the environment moves backward at 0.55 meters/second after a five-second eased start. The initial head orientation defines the journey axis, including when lying down. Forward optic flow always follows that axis; the tracked camera itself is never animated.
