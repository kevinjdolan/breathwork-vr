# Prismatic Sanctuary — flowing geometric trance

Eight minutes of slow forward optic flow through 128 floating chromatic objects and a 32,768-tile relief vault with 8,192 floating filament particles. The tracked camera remains physically correct: the environment moves backward at 0.55 meters/second after a five-second eased start. The initial head orientation defines the journey axis, including when lying down. Two cascaded 3.2-second quaternion filters then turn the complete tunnel toward the current gaze, with an eased onset and approximately 16 seconds to settle after a large turn. Forward optic flow always follows that axis; the tracked camera itself is never animated.

Sixteen mesh families provide different silhouettes and real depth: tetrahedral lantern, cube window, octahedral crystal, icosahedral jewel, dodecahedral lantern, cuboctahedral cage, truncated tetrahedron, pentagonal antiprism, hexagonal bipyramid, stellated octahedron, five-point prism, triangular torus, trefoil knot, Möbius ribbon, double helix, and gyroscopic rings. Faceted frames, recessed interiors, ribbons and intertwined curves use cyan, orange, violet, magenta, mint and amber.

Objects drift and rotate independently. A smaller near lane passes beside the viewer; particles and meshes fade before entering the face exclusion region. Object positions and sizes follow the tunnel’s contraction. Conservative whole-mesh radial bounds keep every shape inside its walls. The objects render before the vault and write depth, so nearby facets occlude particles behind them. Objects recycle through a 64-meter field only after fading out behind the viewer, and fade in at the distant boundary. The 64,064 object triangles, 65,536 relief triangles, 16,384 filament triangles and 640 star triangles are instanced; there is no runtime mesh rebuilding or volumetric raymarching.

A pure white particle light marks the distant center, remaining exactly 60 meters from the tracked head regardless of travel. It stays brilliant through the directional fog, which obscures distant objects and vault particles between 12 and 32 meters. Its radius grows to 1.9 times normal over the first 500 ms of the full breath hold, then shrinks to zero over the first 500 ms of exhalation. It stays absent through the empty pause and returns over the first 500 ms of inhalation. Intensity stays white; the phase cue changes radius rather than dimming it with fog.

Each inhale selects a visible geometric object with a predicted six-second sightline. That object remains locked throughout the cue and takes on a green glow. Two continuous tubular plasma arcs lead from its moving center to the tracked nose, beginning to form 1.5 seconds before inhalation and tapering into the full hold. The tubes are static meshes deformed by the shader; they do not rebuild each frame or increase speed at the phase boundary. Fine cores and broader soft halos preserve a solid path without creating a large near-face glow.

The tunnel radius constricts by 40% over the inhale. During the full hold it brightens with a restrained 90 BPM pulse. During exhalation, 512 blue particles emerge from the mouth, travel forward, then branch right, left, up and down as the tunnel expands. Their fixed birth times and travel duration preserve smooth motion; they clear before the next green lead-in. The lake's original breath streams remain hidden in this world, while particle hands remain active. Thirty complete 4/4/4/4 box-breath cycles fill the session.

The score is one authored 90 BPM electronic arrangement: rounded low kick, soft offbeat bass, filtered shaker and sustained synth chords aligned to the same sample clock. At 48 kHz each beat is exactly 32,000 samples, totaling 720 beats. Four-second chord crossfades soften the 32-beat harmonic changes. The music spans 480 seconds, with eight-second overall fades, mastered to approximately −20 LUFS. It contains no piano, plucked melody or independently timed generated backing music. The previous Lyria-plus-sequencer blend sounded like two competing tracks in headset feedback, so those backing recordings and the bell-like arpeggio were removed. Old Lyria masters remain retained locally. Current provenance is in `audio/prismatic_trance_provenance.json`.

The first integrated render revealed a faint star and sparse vault. The second pass increased the star's angular size, doubled vault density, enlarged its points, improved text legibility and cleared the central sightline. Automated audio review described a gentle, even electronic trance pulse with no detected voices, harsh attacks, startling transitions or ominous tension. Desktop Mobile Vulkan renders validate seated and reclining composition; they do not establish headset comfort or sustained Quest frame rate.

Run the integrated review with `--experience=prismatic_sanctuary --review-start=40 --review-duration=10 --review-path=/absolute/output`, adding `--review-recline` to start looking up. `tests/test_prismatic.gd` checks mesh diversity and bounds, triangle budget, smooth cruise motion, fixed star distance, 500 ms radius transitions, source lock and predicted sightlines, containment through contraction, and sixteen-second gaze following.


## Ornamental relief — version 2.3

The reference images informed original procedural ornament: nested rosettes, interwoven diagonals, curling filigree, occasional almond eyes and rounded four-legged geckos with curling tails. The supplied artwork is not copied into the runtime as a texture. Closely packed instanced tiles form a continuous-looking surface with actual radial folds and recesses, spanning approximately 4.75–8.05 meters before breath contraction. A separate population of small light particles floats just inside that relief. The complete wall follows the existing slow gaze turn, forward travel, breath contraction and directional fog.

The ornament uses a moving material coordinate so it stays attached to passing geometry. Long smooth envelopes introduce eyes and geckos at separate times; palette and fold weights continue changing across the whole eight minutes. Eyes occupy approximately 30% of cells during their passages, geckos approximately 23% during theirs. Expensive gecko shape evaluation is skipped outside those cells and passages. The particle tile shader writes depth so floating objects and breath paths retain spatial occlusion. Four session times and side views were rendered to verify both recognizable motifs and continued variety; a reclining motion capture checks the integrated flow.
